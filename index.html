03 — Projects

Things I've Built

Senior Design Project
Smart Cane
ENR 490 — Engineering Design Project · Bethel University · May 2026

Collaborated with a Computer Engineering major and a Mechanical Engineering major to design a custom-built assistive cane combining obstacle detection, fall detection, and wireless emergency notification with the structural stability of a traditional walking cane — designed, fabricated, and programmed from the ground up for ~$143 in materials.

  • Two ultrasonic sensors detect obstacles up to 7 ft away across 3 distance zones, communicating proximity and left/right direction via independent haptic vibration motors in a custom 3D-printed handle.
  • MPU-6050 accelerometer/gyroscope detects falls; sends emergency notification via HTTP POST to the ntfy mobile app over WiFi if the cane stays horizontal for 5+ seconds.
  • Designed the rechargeable battery pack (18650 Li-ion + TP4056 charger module + MT3608 boost converter) providing a stable 5 V supply to the ESP32 and all peripherals.
  • Designed dual IRLZ44N MOSFET vibration motor circuits with current-limiting gate resistors to suppress voltage ringing, enabling pulse-frequency-coded obstacle alerts.
  • Achieved zero obstacle collisions by the fourth blindfolded trial through a randomly arranged obstacle course.
